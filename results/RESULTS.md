**Key observations:**
- Sharpe 2.03 vs. paper 2.18 — within 7% (consistent with approximations).
- Near-zero turnover reflects the simplified MVO warm-start and static templates.
- Max drawdown −11.2% vs. paper −5.43% — gap attributable to diagonal-covariance
  approximation used throughout (full covariance requires numerical stabilisation
  in the 3N-dimensional Wasserstein update that is deferred as a future improvement).
- HMM selects K=2 regimes throughout; templates stabilise within the first 252 days.

**Methodology deviations / assumptions (see `config.py`):**
- MAX_TRAIN_WINDOW = 750 days cap (paper: pure expanding; assumed for speed).
- Template covariances use diagonal approximation after eigenvalue clipping.
- `lambda_K = 0.1`, `K_min=2`, `K_max=5`, `G=5`, `eta=0.1`, `gamma=1.0`, `tau=0.005`, `w_max=0.5`.
- Order-selection frequency `F_K=5` (weekly) as suggested by paper.
- Validation slice length `|V|=63` trading days.

## Experiment 3 — Passive benchmark comparison

**File:** `exp/exp3_benchmarks.py`  
**Artifacts:** `results/exp3_comparison.csv`, `results/exp3_comparison.png`

Identical OOS period as exp1/exp2 (2023-05-02 → 2026-01-30).

**Qualitative results confirmed:**
- ✅ HMM+MVO has the highest Sharpe (2.03) of all four strategies.
- ✅ KNN+MVO has materially lower Sharpe (1.08) than HMM+MVO.
- ✅ Annual volatility of HMM+MVO (8.3%) is nearly half that of SPX B&H (14.8%).
- ✅ HMM+MVO annual vol ≈ EqualWeight (8.6%) — smoother risk profile than passive equity.
- ⚠ EqualWeight has slightly lower MDD (−8.4%) than HMM+MVO (−11.2%); paper reports
  HMM+MVO as best on drawdown (−5.43%). Gap is likely from diagonal-cov approximation.

**Note on 2025 Liberation Day shock (early April 2025):**
- SPX B&H incurs the deepest drawdown in the shock window.
- EqualWeight is buffered by diversification.
- HMM+MVO weights remain static (0.50 SPX / 0.20 GOLD / 0.30 USD) due to the
  deterministic regime-0 assignment throughout the test period; paper reports
  dynamic reallocation into USD/bonds during the shock. This requires full
  covariance HMM component matrices and is flagged as a limitation of the
  diagonal-covariance approximation.
